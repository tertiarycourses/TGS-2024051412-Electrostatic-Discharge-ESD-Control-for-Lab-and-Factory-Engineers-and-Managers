# Source and Legacy Coverage — v3.0

Course: Electrostatic Discharge (ESD) Control for Lab and Factory Engineers and Managers (TGS-2024051412)

## Legacy coverage retained

- Topic 1: static charge, discharge paths, HBM/MM/CDM, catastrophic/latent failure, manufacturing impact and susceptible devices.
- Topic 2: grounding, bonding, shielding, charge reduction, handling/storage, personnel controls, mats, packaging, ionisation, monitoring, garments, EPA setup and maintenance.
- Topic 3: ANSI/ESD S20.20 and IEC 61340 programme concepts, testing, auditing and metrics.
- Topic 4: employee roles, awareness, incident reporting, CAPA and competence.
- All 88 picture instances from the old PPT are imported with provenance.
