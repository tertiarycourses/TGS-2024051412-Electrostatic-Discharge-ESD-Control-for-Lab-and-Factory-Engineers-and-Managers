# Source and Legacy Coverage — v3.1

Course: Electrostatic Discharge (ESD) Control for Lab and Factory Engineers and Managers (TGS-2024051412)

## Logical learning flow

1. Charge generation, electric fields and discharge paths.
2. HBM, legacy MM and CDM models; physical defects; catastrophic, latent and cumulative outcomes.
3. Reliability escape paths and business risk.
4. Prevention through EPA design, grounding/bonding, personnel controls, ionisation, shielding, packaging and maintenance.
5. ANSI/ESD S20.20-2021 and IEC 61340-5-1:2024 programme structure, qualification, verification, measurement, audit and metrics.
6. Role competence, incident evidence, root-cause analysis, CAPA and management review.

## Important standards boundary

Exact numerical limits, test setups and acceptance methods must come from the organisation's current controlled standards, customer requirements and site procedures. The slides explain the decision system and do not reproduce controlled tables as operating instructions.
