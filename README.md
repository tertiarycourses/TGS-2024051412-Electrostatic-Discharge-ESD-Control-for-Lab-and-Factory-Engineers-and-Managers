## About the course

Engineers and managers learn how charge is generated and discharged, how HBM, MM and CDM events damage devices, and how to design, commission, verify and audit an ESD Protected Area (EPA) against current ESD standards. The course closes with incident response, CAPA and ESD programme management.

- Assessment: one-hour Written Assessment (SAQ) and one-hour Case Study
- Coverage: electrostatic physics; HBM, MM and CDM; physical and latent defects; device protection; reliability; EPA prevention; standards; verification; auditing; incident response; CAPA; and programme management

## Learning outcomes

- LO1: Apply control measures to manage ESD generation based on ESD principles.
- LO2: Evaluate and recommend actions to improve ESD control effectiveness in accordance with ESD standards.

## Topics covered

1. Basics of ESD
2. ESD Control Principles, Equipment and Practices
3. ESD Standards, Testing and Auditing
4. Training, Awareness and Incident Improvement
