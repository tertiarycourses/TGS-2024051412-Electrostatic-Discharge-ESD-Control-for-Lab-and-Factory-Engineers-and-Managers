# Scenario Brief — Activity 4

A robot transfers packaged ICs from plastic tubes to a grounded nest. Operators also perform manual rework. The team proposes more wrist straps, but field readings near the tube exit remain high.

## Your role

- automation engineer
- ESD specialist
- maintenance supervisor

## Constraints

- Work only from the evidence supplied or clearly label assumptions.
- Use the current controlled ESD programme/standard for exact limits.
- Protect product, people and evidence before optimising speed or cost.
