# Scenario Brief — Activity 6

Helix Assembly claims alignment to ANSI/ESD S20.20 and IEC 61340-5-1. A new customer requests evidence that controls are implemented, verified and improved—not merely purchased.

## Your role

- lead auditor
- ESD programme coordinator
- customer observer

## Constraints

- Work only from the evidence supplied or clearly label assumptions.
- Use the current controlled ESD programme/standard for exact limits.
- Protect product, people and evidence before optimising speed or cost.
