# Scenario Brief — Activity 8

Apex Microsystems is opening a mixed lab/factory line for sensitive industrial controllers. Management needs an auditable ESD control plan before customer qualification, covering controls, verification, competence, incidents and improvement.

## Your role

- ESD programme manager
- operations manager
- quality manager
- customer assessor

## Constraints

- Work only from the evidence supplied or clearly label assumptions.
- Use the current controlled ESD programme/standard for exact limits.
- Protect product, people and evidence before optimising speed or cost.
