# Scenario Brief — Activity 3

Orion Optics is moving laser-diode assembly into a small shared lab. The proposed bench has a mat and wrist strap but no documented boundary, common point, insulator policy or readiness test.

## Your role

- ESD engineer
- facilities engineer
- lab manager

## Constraints

- Work only from the evidence supplied or clearly label assumptions.
- Use the current controlled ESD programme/standard for exact limits.
- Protect product, people and evidence before optimising speed or cost.
