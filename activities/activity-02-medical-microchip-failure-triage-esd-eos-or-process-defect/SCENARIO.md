# Scenario Brief — Activity 2

A medical-device ASIC passes wafer test but shows intermittent leakage after final assembly. A handler alarm occurred during the lot, yet the power-supply log also shows a transient. Management wants a defensible triage before quarantined product is released.

## Your role

- reliability engineer
- failure-analysis lead
- production manager

## Constraints

- Work only from the evidence supplied or clearly label assumptions.
- Use the current controlled ESD programme/standard for exact limits.
- Protect product, people and evidence before optimising speed or cost.
