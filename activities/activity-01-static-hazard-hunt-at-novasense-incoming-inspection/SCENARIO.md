# Scenario Brief — Activity 1

NovaSense receives MEMS sensor trays beside a label printer, stretch-wrap station and wheeled inspection trolley. Failure returns increased after a layout change, but no one has mapped charge-generation and induction risks.

## Your role

- ESD engineer
- incoming-inspection supervisor
- quality observer

## Constraints

- Work only from the evidence supplied or clearly label assumptions.
- Use the current controlled ESD programme/standard for exact limits.
- Protect product, people and evidence before optimising speed or cost.
